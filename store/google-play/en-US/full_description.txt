Wisp & Flame is a collection of playful games for couples who want more than another night in front of a screen. Spin, draw, plan and play together, and let each round bring you a little closer.

TWO PATHS, FIVE LEVELS EACH
Choose the mood for tonight:
• Intimacy: Whisper, Closeness, Secrets, Flame, Bond. Tender, slow and emotional.
• Exploration: Spark, Glow, Courage, Summit, Legend. For curious couples who like to be surprised.
Start gently and go further only when you both want to. Every game remembers your choice.

THE GAMES
• Spin the Wheel: let fate pick a category, then a prompt to act on together.
• Cards: pick your favourite categories and draw. Every card is a small invitation.
• Boxes: a shared space for the two of you. Create a room, share the six-character code with your partner and fill boxes with ideas, wishes and plans. Changes sync instantly between both phones.
• Poses: a deck of elegant charcoal sketches. Draw one and discover a new way to be close.
• Glimpse: half-revealed sketches, each with a prompt. Your imagination completes the picture.

MADE FOR TWO
• No sign-up, no profiles, no feed. Open the app and play.
• Hundreds of prompts written for couples, with new ones added regularly.
• Available in 12 languages, including English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Japanese and Korean.
• Send us your own ideas from Settings; the best ones may become new cards.

FOR ADULTS ONLY
Wisp & Flame is intended for consenting adult couples (18+). Always respect each other's boundaries: any prompt can be skipped, and every level is opt-in.
