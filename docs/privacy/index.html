Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Wisp & Flame ("the app") is a set of games for adult couples. It is built and operated by Enes Günel ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and what choices you have. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Data we process

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Anonymous user ID: a random identifier created by Firebase Authentication when you first open the app. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.To let you join a Boxes room and to keep rooms private to their two members.Google Firebase (servers in the EU)
Boxes content: room code, box names, colours and icons, the notes you and your partner add, and confirmation marks.To sync the shared Boxes game between your two phones in real time.Google Firebase (servers in the EU). Readable only by the two members of the room.

Like any internet service, Firebase receives your device's IP address and basic technical information while delivering content. We do not use this data to identify you.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd., to store and deliver data. Our database is hosted in Google's European multi-region. Google processes this data on our behalf under its data processing terms. See also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. How long we keep data

  • Boxes: until you delete them. You can delete boxes and notes in the app at any time. To have a whole room removed, contact us with its room code.
  • Suggestions and fantasies: until we have reviewed them, and at most 24 months.
  • On-device data: until you delete the app.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because the app has no accounts, we can only find your data from details you give us, such as a room code or the text of a message you sent. To make a request, email enesgunel@enesgunel.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Adults only

The app is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google. Access rules on our database ensure that a room's contents can only be read by its two members, and that submitted messages can only be read by us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Enes Günel · enesgunel@enesgunel.com
